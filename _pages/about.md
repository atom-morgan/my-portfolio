I started my career in marketing consulting working with a variety of Fortune 500 companies. My main focus was data analysis, but after coding a few internal tools I eventually decided to quit my job and enter the tech industry.

Since then, I’ve worked in a variety of environments ranging from Fortune 500 companies to startups and creative agencies. Across these companies, I’ve taken on roles spanning full-stack development, UI design, and UX research, spending most of my time building with JavaScript and TypeScript.

Most recently, I spent five years running my own business as an online fitness coach, building custom client pipelines, automating operations, and managing every piece of the stack solo. Watching the tech landscape evolve from the sidelines during that stretch, the recent breakthroughs in AI felt undeniably like Internet 2.0, a shift too compelling to pass up.

Now, I'm channeling that hard-earned entrepreneurial grit and systems-level thinking back into full-stack software development.
